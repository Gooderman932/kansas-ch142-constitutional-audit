## Request

Under the Kansas Open Records Act, K.S.A. 45-215 et seq., I request electronic
copies of the following existing records concerning the Olathe Police
Department response on July 11, 2026 to a call from an ICE officer identified in
public reporting as Jack Ravencamp, at or near the intersection of Harrison
Street and Dennis Avenue in Olathe:

1. The call-for-service, CAD/event record, incident number, event chronology,
   unit assignments, status changes, disposition code, and associated call
   notes.
2. The incoming call recording, dispatcher audio, radio traffic, and any
   available transcript.
3. Incident reports, supplements, field notes, witness statements, citations,
   arrest reports, probable-cause affidavits, and charging referrals.
4. Body-worn-camera and dash-camera recordings from responding Olathe officers,
   including associated metadata and audit history.
5. Records sufficient to identify the responding Olathe officers and the
   outside agencies recorded in the CAD/event record.
6. Communications between Olathe personnel and ICE or the Department of
   Homeland Security concerning the call or incident.
7. Records sufficient to show the retention category, scheduled destruction
   date, and any evidence or litigation hold applied to records responsive to
   items 1 through 6.

This request uses the date, location, caller, and agency to identify the event.
If an incident or CAD number is needed to complete processing, please provide it
under item 1 rather than returning or delaying the request on a technicality.
