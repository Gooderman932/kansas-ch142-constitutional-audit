# Pilot Tranche Routing

Verified September 3, 2026. This file identifies where each draft should go;
it does not record that any request was transmitted.

## State and incident-specific requests

- **Kansas Attorney General (`20260903-KSAG-CH142-001`)**: Address `KORA-01` to
  the Official Custodian of Records, Office of the Kansas Attorney General, 120
  SW 10th Ave., 2nd Floor, Topeka, KS 66612-1597. No dedicated KORA inbox or
  request form was published, so call (785) 296-2215 to confirm routing before
  mailing. Sources: [Open Government](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government),
  [KORA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/kora-faq),
  and [Contact Us](https://www.ag.ks.gov/about-us/contact-us).
- **Olathe Police (`20260903-OLATHE-PD-CH142-001`)**: Submit `KORA-04` through
  the [City of Olathe public-records portal](https://olatheks-self.govplatform.com/service/Public_Records_Request)
  and mail a parallel copy to Olathe Police Department Records Division, 501 E.
  56 Hwy., Olathe, KS 66061. Confirm at 913-971-7500. The Police Records page
  says fax delivery does not include video, audio, or photographs. Sources:
  [Police Records](https://www.olatheks.gov/government/police/records) and
  [City Clerk public-records guidance](https://www.olatheks.gov/government/city-clerk/public-records-requests).

## Five-county pilot

### Cherokee County

- **Sheriff and jail records (`20260903-CHEROKEE-SO-CH142-001`)**: Send the
  operational portions of `KORA-02` in writing to the Administrative Assistant
  / KORA Officer, Cherokee County Sheriff's Office, P.O. Box 479, 915 East
  Country Road, Columbus, KS 66725. Confirm receipt at 620-429-3992. The agency
  does not publish a direct records email.
- **County-government records (`20260903-CHEROKEE-CLERK-CH142-001`)**: Send the
  fiscal, insurance, commission, and oversight portions using the county form
  to Shelby.Robinson@cherokeecounty-ks.gov, with
  rebecca.brassart@cherokeecounty-ks.gov copied. The Clerk's address is 110 W.
  Maple St., Room 131, Columbus, KS 66725.
- Sources: [Sheriff administration](https://www.cherokeecountykssheriff.com/administration),
  [Sheriff FAQ](https://www.cherokeecountykssheriff.com/faq), and
  [county KORA form](https://cherokeecountyks.gov/main/images/KORA_-_Clerks_Request_Form.pdf).

### Johnson County

- **Sheriff and detention records (`20260903-JOHNSON-SO-CH142-001`)**: Email
  `KORA-02` to `SHR-Recordrequests@jocogov.org`, Central Records Division.
  Mailing backup: Johnson County Sheriff's Office, c/o Central Records Division,
  27747 W. 159th St., New Century, KS 66031. Confirm at 913-715-5460.
- Source: [Johnson County Sheriff's Records](https://www.jocogov.org/johnson-county-sheriff/public-information/sheriffs-records).

### Sedgwick County

- **County FIO (`20260903-SEDGWICK-FIO-CH142-001`)**: Email `KORA-02` to Jennifer
  Scriven, Records Manager and Freedom of Information Officer, at
  `kora-fio@sedgwick.gov`, or use the county form. State expressly that the
  request includes Sheriff's Office and detention-facility records. Include the
  requester's physical mailing address, which the county says is required.
- Source: [Sedgwick County open-records request page](https://www.sedgwickcounty.org/kora/request-sedgwick-county-government-records/open-records-request/).

### Shawnee County

- **Sheriff records (`20260903-SHAWNEE-SO-CH142-001`)**: Send the section 5
  enforcement portion of `KORA-02` using the Sheriff's published form to
  `SNSORecordsRequests@snco.us`. Mailing backup: Shawnee County Sheriff's
  Office, Attn: Request for Records, 320 S. Kansas Ave., Suite 200, Topeka, KS
  66603.
- **Jail and county records (`20260903-SHAWNEE-CLERK-CH142-001`)**: Route the
  detention, fiscal, insurance, commission, and oversight portions through the
  County Clerk's form. Ask the Clerk to forward detention items to the
  Department of Corrections or identify its custodian; the adult jail is not
  operated by the Sheriff.
- Sources: [Sheriff Request for Records](https://www.shawneesheriff.org/sh/request_for_records.php),
  [Sheriff request form](https://cdn.snco.gov/sheriff/document/Kansas_Open_Records_Request.pdf),
  and [County Clerk KORA page](https://www.snco.gov/clerk/kora.php).

### Wyandotte County

- **Sheriff and detention records (`20260903-WYANDOTTE-SO-CH142-001`)**: Email
  the operational portions of `KORA-02` to `oru@wycosheriff.org` and submit a
  mirror request through NextRequest.
- **Unified Government records (`20260903-WYANDOTTE-UG-CH142-001`)**: Submit the
  fiscal, commission, contract, insurance, and oversight portions through
  [NextRequest](https://wycokck.nextrequest.com/). Mailing backup: Clerk's
  Division, 701 N. 7th St., Suite 323, Kansas City, KS 66101.
- Sources: [Wyandotte County Sheriff's Office](https://www.wycokck.org/Departments/Sheriffs-Office)
  and [Unified Government record requests](https://www.wycokck.org/Departments/Clerks-Office/Request-a-Record).

## Review and transmission status

All entries are drafts. Requester identity, physical mailing address, signature,
and delivery authorization remain intentionally blank. No request may be sent
until those fields are completed and the exact outgoing payload is approved.
