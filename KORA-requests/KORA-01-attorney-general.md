## Subject

Kansas Open Records Act request concerning implementation of L. 2026, ch. 142
(Senate Substitute for HB 2372)

## Request

Under the Kansas Open Records Act, K.S.A. 45-215 et seq., I request electronic
copies of the existing records described below. Unless an item states otherwise,
the time period is April 8, 2026 through the date this request is received.

1. All final or operative guidance, legal bulletins, memoranda, model policies,
   training materials, presentations, frequently asked questions, or
   instructions created or distributed by the Office concerning L. 2026, ch.
   142 or Senate Substitute for HB 2372.
2. Records sufficient to identify each state or local agency, association, or
   public official to whom the Office distributed the records described in item
   1, including the distribution date.
3. All requests for advice, interpretation, training, or guidance received by
   the Office concerning section 1, section 2, section 3, section 5, section 9,
   section 10, or section 11 of L. 2026, ch. 142, together with any final
   response. Legal advice or attorney work product may be redacted or withheld
   to the extent authorized by a specifically cited provision of law; please
   produce nonprivileged transmittal, routing, date, sender, recipient, and
   subject-matter information that is reasonably segregable.
4. Records sufficient to identify every request that the Attorney General
   provide legal representation under section 3 of L. 2026, ch. 142, including
   the requesting person or agency, date received, matter or case number, court,
   and disposition of the request. Personally identifying information may be
   redacted where specifically authorized by law.
5. Records sufficient to identify every claim, demand, judgment, settlement, or
   payment presented to or handled by the State under section 2 of L. 2026, ch.
   142, including the amount requested, amount paid, agency involved, matter or
   case number, and disposition.
6. Records sufficient to show all Office expenditures, encumbrances, outside
   counsel expenses, staff-time accounting, or reimbursement payments tied to
   sections 2 or 3 of L. 2026, ch. 142.
7. All final or operative policies, charging guidance, training materials, or
   communications concerning the new crime titled "unlawful approach of a first
   responder" in section 5 of L. 2026, ch. 142.
8. Records sufficient to identify complaints, referrals, or reported incidents
   received by the Office concerning enforcement or threatened enforcement of
   section 5 of L. 2026, ch. 142. This item does not seek victim-identifying
   information or confidential-source information.
9. All records-retention schedules or Office instructions that specifically
   govern the records responsive to items 1 through 8.

## Format, segregation, and timing

Please provide records electronically in their native format when reasonably
available, preserving ordinary metadata. PDF is acceptable for records that do
not have a useful native format.

Please treat each numbered item as severable. If any portion is withheld,
produce all reasonably segregable open material and identify, item by item, the
specific statutory provision authorizing the withholding. This request does not
ask the Office to create a new record, conduct legal research, or answer
interrogatories.

K.S.A. 45-218(d) requires the request to be acted upon as soon as possible and
not later than the end of the third business day after receipt. If production
cannot be completed then, please provide the detailed cause for delay and the
place and earliest time and date the records will be available.
