## Subject

Preservation notice concerning `[INCIDENT OR CASE NUMBER]`

Please preserve existing records concerning the incident identified above. This
is a preservation notice, not a request that the agency create a record and not,
by itself, a demand for immediate production.

The records to be preserved include:

1. Body-worn-camera and dash-camera recordings, including pre-event buffer,
   complete original files, associated audio, metadata, audit history, and any
   redacted or exported copy.
2. Dispatch audio, 911 recordings, radio traffic, CAD/event records, call notes,
   timestamps, unit assignments, status changes, and location data.
3. Photographs, video, audio, drone footage, fixed-camera footage, ALPR alerts,
   and other digital evidence collected or received.
4. Incident reports, supplements, field notes, citations, notices to appear,
   arrest reports, probable-cause affidavits, booking records, evidence logs,
   charging referrals, and disposition records.
5. Agency email, text messages, chat messages, mobile-data-terminal messages,
   and other communications concerning the incident.
6. Records identifying every officer, employee, first responder, agency, and
   outside organization involved in or notified about the incident.
7. Applicable policies, training materials, directives, retention schedules,
   and automatic-deletion settings in effect on the incident date.
8. Records of access, review, copying, export, redaction, alteration, deletion,
   retention-category changes, or litigation/evidence holds applied to any item
   above.

Please suspend routine deletion, overwriting, recycling, or alteration of these
records while preservation obligations are evaluated. Please confirm receipt
and identify the person responsible for implementing the preservation hold.
