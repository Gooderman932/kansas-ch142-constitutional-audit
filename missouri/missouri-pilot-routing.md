## Notes

- **One request, one custodian.** Unlike Kansas, where Cherokee / Shawnee /
  Wyandotte were split sheriff + clerk, Missouri pilot requests are split
  by agency, not by topic. A county sheriff gets MO-02 (detainer / 287(g) /
  § 574.207). A police department gets MO-03 (ALPR) and, for Joplin only,
  MO-04 (incident). Do not send a combined sheriff + police letter.
- **MSHP is two requests in one envelope:** MO-02 (287(g) / EO 25-04) and
  MO-03 (ALPR / EO 26-18 / DPS guidance). Send them as two separate
  requests with two IDs, or as one request with two numbered sections —
  decide at send time, and log it as two tracker rows either way, so the
  287(g) and ALPR responses can be scored separately.
- **KCPD turnaround:** 24 weeks mailed, 12–16 weeks online, per KCPD's own
  page. That is production time, not the § 610.023.3 response clock. The
  tracker must record both: date of the 3-business-day response, and date
  production completes.
- **90-day fee clock:** § 610.026.2 — if a fee demand or a clarification
  request goes unanswered for 90 days (150 days if fees exceed $1,000),
  the request is considered withdrawn. Calendar that date the day a fee
  demand arrives.
- **Before any send:** add the tracker row first, fill `[DATE]` with the
  actual send date, confirm the custodian, and get Matt's approval of the
  exact payload and destination. That is the same guardrail as Kansas.
