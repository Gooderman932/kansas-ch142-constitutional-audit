## Subject

Missouri Sunshine Law request concerning § 574.207, 287(g) coordination, and Executive Order 26-18 (ALPR)

## Request

Under the Missouri Sunshine Law, Chapter 610, RSMo, I request electronic
copies of the existing records described below. I request existing records,
not answers to questions or newly created analysis.

Unless an item states otherwise, the time period is 28 August 2025 through
the date this request is received. For 287(g) items the period is
13 January 2025 through the date this request is received. For ALPR items
the period is 16 September 2026 through the date this request is received.

### § 574.207 — Interference with a first responder

1. All final or operative guidance, legal bulletins, memoranda, model
   policies, training materials, presentations, frequently asked questions,
   or instructions created or distributed by the Office concerning
   § 574.207, RSMo (L. 2025, H.B. 225), including guidance on the
   First Amendment savings clause in § 574.207.4.
2. Records sufficient to identify each state or local agency, association,
   or public official to whom the Office distributed the records described
   in item 1, including the distribution date.
3. All requests for advice, interpretation, training, or guidance received
   by the Office concerning § 574.207, together with any final response.
   Legal advice or attorney work product may be redacted or withheld to
   the extent authorized by a specifically cited provision of law; please
   produce nonprivileged transmittal, routing, date, sender, recipient,
   and subject-matter information that is reasonably segregable.
4. Records sufficient to identify complaints, referrals, or reported
   incidents received by the Office concerning enforcement or threatened
   enforcement of § 574.207. This item does not seek victim-identifying
   information or confidential-source information.

### 287(g) and immigration enforcement

5. All final or operative guidance, memoranda, or instructions concerning
   Executive Order 25-04, § 43.032, RSMo, or a memorandum of agreement
   under 8 U.S.C. § 1357(g), including the Missouri State Highway Patrol
   memorandum of agreement signed 12 March 2025.
6. Records sufficient to identify every request that the Office provide
   legal representation or advice concerning a 287(g) agreement, an
   immigration-detainer hold, or a claim arising from immigration
   enforcement, including the requesting person or agency, date received,
   matter or case number, court, and disposition.
7. Records sufficient to identify every claim, demand, judgment,
   settlement, or payment presented to or handled by the State concerning
   immigration enforcement or a detainer hold, including the amount
   requested, amount paid, agency involved, matter or case number, and
   disposition.
8. All records concerning Executive Order 25-05 and the collection of
   immigration status in the statewide uniform crime data reporting system.

### ALPR — Executive Order 26-18

9. All final or operative guidance, memoranda, or instructions concerning
   Executive Order 26-18 (16 September 2026) or the Department of Public
   Safety ALPR Best Practices Guide (30 September 2026), including any
   legislative proposal developed under § II of the Order.
10. Records sufficient to identify complaints, referrals, or reported
    incidents received by the Office concerning ALPR misuse, including
    the Joplin Police Department matter referred to the Missouri State
    Highway Patrol for criminal investigation in January 2026, to the
    extent the Office holds records concerning it.

### Administration

11. Records sufficient to show all Office expenditures, encumbrances,
    outside counsel expenses, staff-time accounting, or reimbursement
    payments tied to items 1 through 10.
12. All records-retention schedules or Office instructions that
    specifically govern the records responsive to items 1 through 11.
