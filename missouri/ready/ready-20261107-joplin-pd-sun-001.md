## Why this is a separate module, not part of MO-02 / MO-03

MO-02 asks for § 574.207 counts and 287(g) records. MO-03 asks for ALPR
contracts, policy, retention, sharing, and logs in general. MO-04 asks for
one dated incident, one named agency, and the investigation that followed
it. That specificity is what makes a preservation / incident request
answerable, and it is also what makes it sensitive: the investigation may
still be closed under § 610.100, and personnel records may be closed under
§ 610.021. The sendable letter must ask for the log and the policy first,
and for the investigation file only to the extent it is open, with the
item-by-item basis required by § 610.024.
